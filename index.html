SEEDS seminar
Grad Social, Earth, and Environmental Data Science Seminar  ·  Stanford
What?
A space for junior researchers — graduate students, post-docs, and pre-docs — to discuss quantitative environmental social science at all stages and get feedback in a supportive environment. Pitch an early-stage idea, talk through something you're stuck on, or practice a conference talk.
Who?
Open to grad students, post-docs, and pre-docs across GEP, ESoS, E-IPER, and FSE. Faculty from GEP / ESoS / ESS are invited to some meetings. Regularly attending? You're agreeing to present at some point!
When?
Thursdays, 12:00–1:00 pm, every 2–3 weeks.
Format?
Format varies between longer 45–50 min presentations, two shorter 25–30 min presentations, or 5-min research pitches, with time for questions, discussion, and feedback. Faculty attend some sessions, which are marked Faculty. The rest are pre-doc/grad/post-doc only.
Food?
Lunch is provided — sign up below so we include you in the headcount.
